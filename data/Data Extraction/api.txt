Save & re-submit:
PUT https://supplier.uat1.ligentix.net/Api/Shipments/shipment/SubmitShipment
Request:
(same structure as shipment/Details endpoint json)
Response:
{"isSuccess":true,"errors":null}
Afterwards ShipmentInformation and shipment/Details endpoints will be called again. bookingSearch is not called.
